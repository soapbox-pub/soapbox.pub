## **How To Listen: We Used Soapbox Tools to Build Our Own Podcast App**

Now that we’re entering an age of being able to build with AI and absolutely no writing of code, we decided to see if we could launch a podcast with a podcasting app we built ourselves. The result is Podstr, an app Derek Ross built using MKStack.

**«photo of Podstr»**

To listen to Soapbox Sessions, you can give Podstr a spin. For the best experience, sign up for Nostr and log in. Then you can comment and become part of our community. Empowering communities to form, grow, and thrive in the decentralized web is one of the key goals of our mission.

You can also listen to Soapbox Sessions wherever you get your podcasts. We’ll even post the video version on our YouTube channel.

## **If We Can Do It, You Can Do it**

By using MKStack to build Podstr, we created a way to host our podcast by using our own Soapbox technology. Fun fact: neither one of us is a software developer. Derek, being our DevRel, naturally took the path towards creating Podstr by prompting in MKStack. It helps you build powerful Nostr applications with AI-first development. Install it and use in your terminal. 

If you’re not a developer and have no idea what a terminal even is, we have Shakespeare for you\! It’s our conversational AI builder anyone can use, especially if you have no technical knowledge at all. This is what Heather likes to use to build websites and apps. 

Soapbox Sessions is self-hosted and we use Podcasting 2.0 technology like wallet splits, or value time splits. This means that if people send value to our podcast in the form of a payment, it can be split between our hosts, Soapbox, [Ainsley Costello](https://ainsleycostello.com/) who recorded our intro, and our charity of choice, The Human Rights Foundation (HRF). This will help us cover server costs, pay Ainsley, and help HRF\! 

*Learn about this idea in [Podcasting 2.0](https://value4value.info/pieces/) and about [Value Time Splits for developers](https://podcastindex-org.github.io/docs-api/#get-/recent/soundbites).*
