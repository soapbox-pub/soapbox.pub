Blobbi Virtual Pets

Meet your Blobbi. A virtual pet that lives right inside Ditto. Hatch a mysterious egg, name your creature, and watch it evolve into one of 16 different adult forms. Feed it, clean it, heal it, play music for it. Complete daily quests, earn rewards, and collect items from the shop. Your Blobbi even follows you around the app.

Hatch Your Blobbi →

Letters

Letters brings personal, decorated messaging to Ditto. Pick custom stationery, choose a font, add emoji stickers, and even attach a theme as a gift. The recipient gets a 3D interactive envelope they can flip open. It's the most personal way to reach someone on any social platform.

Try Letters →

In-App Article Editor

Write long-form content with a full rich-text editor built right into Ditto. Formatting toolbar, inline images, auto-saving drafts, and cloud sync. Toggle between rich text and markdown. It's everything you need to publish without leaving the app.

Try the Article Editor →

Badges & Custom Emoji

Badges display on profiles and can be awarded to other users. Create your own, award them with a multi-select picker, and reorder your showcase with drag-and-drop. Custom emoji packs got a full upgrade too, with a new creator, drag-and-drop uploads, and shortcode autocomplete everywhere.

Explore Badges → Browse Emoji Packs →

Nsites & Nostr Apps

Run nsites and web apps directly inside Ditto. Hit the "Run" button on any nsite card and it opens in an overlay without leaving your feed. Build with Shakespeare, deploy to Nostr, and anyone on Ditto can discover and launch it from their timeline.

Explore Nsites →

Plus So Much More

Follow links and QR codes for easy sharing. Follow packs to onboard communities. Curated home feeds. Poll upgrades. Customizable navigation. Full threaded replies. Pull-to-refresh. A React 19 upgrade with ~50% smaller bundles. And dozens of bug fixes.
