3 Places You Can Make a Difference with Agora Today

We built Agora for the people the financial system leaves behind. No middlemen, no platform fees, no campaign shutdowns. Just Bitcoin, sent directly to a wallet the recipient controls.

Here are three places around the world where your sats can make a difference today.

🇵🇸 Gaza, Palestine

A father starting over after a middleman took everything

Sami Shannat raised more than £55,000 on GoFundMe to evacuate his family from Gaza. The platform doesn't pay out in Gaza, so the funds went to a beneficiary in the UK. Sami says that middleman kept the money, over $65,000 across his family's campaigns. Now he's starting over on Agora, where every donation goes straight to a wallet only he controls.

Sami's story was also featured in Forbes this week, alongside Agora's work getting Bitcoin to people in crisis. That one's worth a read too.

🇰🇪 Matangi, Kenya

A circular economy built for women's sovereignty

We visited Bitcoin Babies in Matangi, Kenya, where mothers receive weekly stipends in Bitcoin and spend them at local shops, clinics, and markets that all accept sats over Lightning. Many of the merchants are graduates of the program themselves. The money circulates instead of leaking out, and mothers get money no one else can freeze, take, or spend for them.

🇻🇪 Venezuela

Earthquake relief with no NGO in between

When an earthquake hit Venezuela, foreign aid flowed through big NGOs while the people actually in the shelters were locked out of fundraising for themselves. Local organizers turned to Agora to raise money directly from donors worldwide. The collective relief fund distributes your donation across all the earthquake relief campaigns on the ground.
