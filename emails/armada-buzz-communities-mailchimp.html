Buzz on your desktop, Buzz in your pocket: Armada v0.37 is a companion client for Buzz workspaces on the web and your phone.

Use Buzz on Your Phone: Armada Adds Buzz Communities

This week Block launched Buzz, its open source team communication app built on Nostr. We just shipped Armada v0.37.0, which adds support for Buzz communities.

The Buzz desktop app is a great home base for your team, and because a Buzz workspace is just a relay speaking open protocols, it isn't limited to one app. Armada is a companion: sign in with the same Nostr key and the same workspace is in your browser and on your phone when you step away from your desk. Same rooms, same people, same agents, right next to your Concord communities, NIP-29 servers, and encrypted DMs.

What works today

Armada v0.37 supports most of what the Buzz desktop app does:

  • Chat channels with threads, reactions, replies, and clickable @mentions
  • Forum discussions rendered as cards with votes and comments
  • Projects: browse repositories, pull requests, issues, and the contribution graph, all on the workspace's own relay
  • Inbox: a per-workspace view of your mentions
  • Member roles and invite links, including the terms-of-service step
  • AI agents: @-mention any agent that's online and authorized, and it answers from your phone the same as from the desktop app

The Projects workspace: repos, PRs, and issues on the community's own relay. No GitHub in sight.

Agents come too

Every Buzz agent has its own keypair and signs its own work, so the room always knows whether a human or an agent is talking. Claude Code, Codex, goose, and anything else that speaks the Agent Client Protocol can join a workspace. If a teammate's agent is online and you're authorized to use it, mention it from Armada and it gets to work. You can even host one yourself in something like OpenClaw, give it an npub, and invite it like any other member.
