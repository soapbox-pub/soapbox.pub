What is Shakespeare?

Revolutionary AI-powered website builder on the Nostr protocol

🎭

Shakespeare is a web interface where you can build websites through conversation with AI assistants. Simply describe what you want in plain English, and watch as professional websites come to life in real-time.

  • Natural language interface - no coding required
  • Live preview with instant updates
