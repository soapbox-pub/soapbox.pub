The Hub for Other Stuff

Most Nostr clients do posts and maybe zaps. Ditto renders everything — all the weird and wonderful things people are building on the protocol.

Photos. Short videos. Events. Polls. Books. Geocaching. Treasure hunts. Follow packs. Color moments. Magic decks. If someone built it on Nostr, you can probably find it on Ditto.

Make It Yours

Custom themes with colors, background images, and fonts. Browse what others have made, remix them, or build your own from scratch. Your profile, your rules.

One App, Three Networks

Ditto bridges Nostr, Mastodon, and Bluesky. Follow and interact with people across all three networks from a single account. Zero lock-in.

Nostr

Your home protocol. Keys you own, relays you choose.

Mastodon

Via Mostr Bridge. The entire Fediverse, in your feed.

Bluesky

Via Bridgy Fed. Follow Bluesky users directly.
